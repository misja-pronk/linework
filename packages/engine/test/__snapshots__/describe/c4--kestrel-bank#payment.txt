Making a transfer

C4 model: From the app to the ledger and back to the phone. 9 elements in 2 groups, with 8 connections.

Groups and elements
- Personal customer (Person): Holds current and savings accounts at Kestrel Bank
- Kestrel Online (Software System): Lets customers check balances, move money and manage their cards
  - Mobile app (Container: Kotlin and Swift): Balances, transfers and cards on the phone
  - Banking API (Container: Spring Boot): Signs customers in and serves accounts and payments as JSON
    - Payments controller (Component: Spring MVC): Checks and submits transfers
    - Ledger client (Component: Spring bean): Reads and books on the core ledger
    - Event publisher (Component: Spring Cloud Stream): Announces what happened to an account
  - Account events (Container: Kafka): Payments and sign-ins as they happen
  - Notifier (Container: Go): Turns account events into emails and push messages
- Core ledger (Software System): Holds every account and books every transaction
- Push gateway (Software System, external): Delivers notifications to iOS and Android

Connections
- Personal customer connects to Mobile app (Confirms a transfer).
- Mobile app connects to Payments controller (Submits it [JSON/HTTPS]).
- Payments controller connects to Ledger client (Books it) and connects to Event publisher (Reports it).
- Ledger client connects to Core ledger (Posts the booking [ISO 20022]).
- Event publisher connects to Account events (Publishes PaymentMade [Kafka]).
- Account events connects to Notifier (Delivers the event).
- Notifier connects to Push gateway (Sends a push [HTTPS]).

Steps
1. Personal customer → Mobile app: Confirms a transfer.
2. Mobile app → Payments controller: Submits it [JSON/HTTPS].
3. Payments controller → Ledger client: Books it.
4. Ledger client → Core ledger: Posts the booking [ISO 20022].
5. Payments controller → Event publisher: Reports it.
6. Event publisher → Account events: Publishes PaymentMade [Kafka].
7. Account events → Notifier: Delivers the event.
8. Notifier → Push gateway: Sends a push [HTTPS].
