Engineering access

Roles, groups and access packages diagram: What each Azure role may do, its own permissions and the ones it includes. A table of 3 rows by 4 columns.

By row (Role by permission)
Columns: read, write, deploy and manage access.
- Reader: read: yes
- Contributor (includes Reader): read: yes, inherited; write: yes; deploy: yes
- Owner (includes Contributor): read: yes, inherited; write: yes, inherited; deploy: yes, inherited; manage access: yes

Key
- Indigo check mark: Granted by the role
- Indigo outlined check mark: Included from another role
