Walkthrough
1. Catalogs: Finance keeps its packages in its own catalog. Partner access lives in a separate catalog.
2. Basics, automatically: Everyone in Finance gets the team, the intranet and the ERP without asking, and loses them when they leave Finance.
3. More rights, two approvals: Key users are approved by their manager and then by the Finance controller. The access ends after 180 days; a quarterly review can remove it sooner.
4. Guests: Fabrikam's auditors request access themselves. The Finance controller approves, it is read-only, and it ends after 90 days.
  - Sam Okafor: Blocked when the last package ends, removed 30 days later
