Sales domain

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind lakehouse · Salesforce, orders and customers into sell_through, for Power BI and Contoso. 14 elements in 2 groups, with 13 connections.

Groups and elements
- Salesforce (Sales Cloud): Accounts and opportunities
- salesforce-ingest (Salesforce connector)
- sales_prod (Catalog)
  - bronze (Schema)
  - silver (Schema)
  - gold (Schema)
    - sell_through (Table)
- bi-warehouse (Serverless SQL warehouse)
- Power BI (Fabric capacity F64): Reports on gold through the SQL warehouse
- Business analysts (Users): Read gold data in Power BI and Genie
- sales-pipeline (Lakeflow pipeline)
- orders_prod (Catalog)
- contoso_sell_through (OpenSharing)
- contoso (Databricks-to-Databricks)
- Contoso Retail (External system): Retail partner; reads sell-through in its own Databricks account
- customers_prod (Catalog)

Connections
- Salesforce sends data to salesforce-ingest (API).
- salesforce-ingest sends data to bronze.
- bronze sends data to silver (clean).
- silver sends data to gold (join).
- gold sends data to bi-warehouse.
- bi-warehouse sends data to Power BI (DirectQuery).
- Power BI sends data to Business analysts (Reports).
- sales-pipeline connects to sales_prod (Management: refreshes).
- orders_prod sends data to gold (orders).
- sell_through sends data to contoso_sell_through (Shared in).
- contoso_sell_through connects to contoso (Granted to).
- contoso sends data to Contoso Retail (OpenSharing).
- customers_prod sends data to gold (customers).

Key
- Solid line: Connection
- Solid violet line with ticks: Data flow
- Dotted line with ticks: Management
