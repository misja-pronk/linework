Incident handling

Process diagram: From the first alert to the review, and who steps in when it is a major one. 12 elements in 4 lanes, with 13 connections.

Lanes and steps
- Monitoring (lane)
  - Alert fires (signal start event)
- On-call engineer (lane)
  - Acknowledge page
  - Assess impact
  - Major incident? (decision decision)
  - Mitigate (roll back or fail over)
  - Service restored? (decision decision)
  - Close incident
- Incident lead (lane)
  - Declare incident
  - Escalate to owning team
  - Write review (within five working days)
  - Incident closed (end event)
- Communications (lane)
  - Post status update (status page and customers)

Main path
1. Alert fires
2. Acknowledge page
3. Assess impact
4. Major incident?
5. Mitigate: Major incident? no
6. Service restored?
7. Close incident: Service restored? yes
8. Write review
9. Incident closed

Connections
- Alert fires leads to Acknowledge page.
- Acknowledge page leads to Assess impact.
- Assess impact leads to Major incident?
- Major incident? leads to Mitigate (no) and leads to Declare incident (yes).
- Mitigate leads to Service restored?
- Declare incident leads to Post status update.
- Service restored? leads to Close incident (yes) and leads to Escalate to owning team (no).
- Post status update leads to Mitigate.
- Close incident leads to Write review.
- Escalate to owning team leads to Mitigate.
- Write review leads to Incident closed.

Key
- Solid line, thick: Happy path
- Solid line: Other paths
