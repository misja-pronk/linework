Harbour Online — containers

C4 model. 7 elements in 1 group, with 8 connections.

Groups and elements
- Retail customer (Person): Banks online and on the phone
- Harbour Online (Software System): Balances, transfers and cards for retail customers
  - Web banking (Container: React)
  - Mobile app (Container: Swift and Kotlin)
  - Banking API (Container: Spring Boot): Serves accounts and payments as JSON
  - Customer store (Container: PostgreSQL 16)
- Core ledger (Software System): Holds every account and books every transaction
- Payments hub (Software System): Checks, routes and settles every payment

Connections
- Retail customer connects to Web banking (Banks online [HTTPS]) and connects to Mobile app (Banks on the phone).
- Web banking connects to Banking API (Calls [JSON/HTTPS]).
- Mobile app connects to Banking API (Calls [JSON/HTTPS]).
- Banking API connects to Customer store (Reads logins [JDBC]), connects to Core ledger (Reads balances) and connects to Payments hub (Submits payments [gRPC]).
- Payments hub connects to Core ledger (Books).
