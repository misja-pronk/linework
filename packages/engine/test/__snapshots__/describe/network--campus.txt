Campus network

Network diagram: Collapsed core, two buildings, distribution and access layers. 12 elements in 4 groups, with 11 connections.

Groups and elements
- Internet and WAN
  - Dual ISP
- Head office campus (10.30.0.0/16)
  - fw-campus (FortiGate 400F · HA pair, a redundant pair)
  - core-01/02 (Catalyst 9500 · 10.30.0.1 · HA SVL, a redundant pair)
  - Building North
    - dist-north (Catalyst 9300 · 10.30.1.1 · HA stack, a redundant pair)
    - acc-n1 (Catalyst 9200 · 48 ports)
    - acc-n2 (Catalyst 9200 · 48 ports)
    - APs north (12× CW9166)
    - Desks north (Clients)
  - Building South
    - dist-south (Catalyst 9300 · 10.30.2.1 · HA stack, a redundant pair)
    - acc-s1 (Catalyst 9200 · 48 ports)
    - APs south (8× CW9166)
    - Lab racks (Server)

Connections
- Dual ISP connects to fw-campus (WAN: 2×10G).
- fw-campus connects to core-01/02 (Trunk: 2×40G).
- core-01/02 connects to dist-north (Fibre: 2×40G) and connects to dist-south (Fibre: 2×40G).
- dist-north connects to acc-n1 (Fibre: 2×10G) and connects to acc-n2 (Fibre: 2×10G).
- dist-south connects to acc-s1 (Fibre: 2×10G).
- acc-n1 is linked with APs north (mGig PoE).
- acc-n2 is linked with Desks north (1G).
- acc-s1 is linked with APs south (mGig PoE) and is linked with Lab racks (10G).

Key
- Solid line with beads: Ethernet
- Solid orange line: Fibre
- Solid indigo line, double, thick: Trunk
- Solid sky line with ticks: WAN
- Solid line, thick: Aggregated link (2× or more)
