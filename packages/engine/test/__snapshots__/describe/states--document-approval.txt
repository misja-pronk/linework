Document approval

States diagram: An architecture decision record from first draft to superseded. 9 states, with 10 transitions.

Elements
- Start
- Draft: do / author writes
- In review: entry / notify the reviewers; do / reviewers comment
- Withdrawn
- All agree?
- End
- Approved: entry / assign a number; do / announce it
- Superseded
- Kept for the record and linked to its successor (note)

Main path
1. Start
2. Draft
3. In review: submitted
4. All agree?: review closed
5. Approved: [yes]
6. Superseded: replaced
7. End

Transitions
- Start moves to Draft.
- Draft moves to In review on submitted and moves to Withdrawn on withdrawn.
- In review moves to All agree? on review closed.
- Withdrawn moves to End.
- All agree? moves to Draft on [changes asked] and moves to Approved on [yes].
- Approved moves to Superseded on replaced.
- Superseded moves to End.
- Kept for the record and linked to its successor is a note on Superseded.
