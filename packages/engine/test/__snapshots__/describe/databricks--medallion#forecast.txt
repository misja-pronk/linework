Demand forecast

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind lakehouse · trained weekly on orders and stock, served to replenishment. 6 elements in 3 groups, with 5 connections.

Groups and elements
- orders_prod (Catalog)
  - gold (Schema)
- forecast-training (Weekly · MLflow)
- supply_prod (Catalog)
  - gold (Schema)
    - demand_forecast (Model)
    - stock_levels (Table)
- demand-forecast (Serving endpoint)
- Replenishment (Supply planning app): Orders stock from suppliers on the demand forecast

Connections
- gold (Schema) sends data to forecast-training (orders).
- forecast-training sends data to demand_forecast (Registers).
- demand_forecast sends data to demand-forecast (Served by).
- demand-forecast sends data to Replenishment (Forecast [REST]).
- gold (Schema) sends data to forecast-training (stock).

Key
- Solid violet line with ticks: Data flow
