Events through Service Bus

Sequence, whole sequence: Northwind orders · one event, two subscribers, each retried on its own, and what fails for good waits in the dead-letter queue. 7 participants and 17 messages.

Participants
- Orders API (App Service)
- Service Bus sb-northwind-prod
  - order-events (topic)
  - Dead-letter queue (per subscription)
- Invoicing (Azure Function)
- Warehouse (Container App)
- Azure Monitor (alert rule)
- Operations

Message flow
In order, top to bottom.
- Publish and deliver:
- 1. Orders API sends to order-events: OrderPlaced, MessageId = order id.
- In parallel: Subscription invoicing
  - 2. order-events sends to Invoicing: OrderPlaced (peek-lock).
  - 3. Invoicing does: Invoice, unless order id seen.
  - 4. Invoicing replies to order-events: Complete.
- And: Subscription warehouse
  - 5. order-events sends to Warehouse: OrderPlaced (peek-lock).
  - 6. Warehouse does: Stock down, retry with backoff.
  - 7. Warehouse replies to order-events: Abandon.
  - 8. order-events sends to Warehouse: Redelivered, DeliveryCount 2 to 10.
  - 9. order-events sends to Dead-letter queue: 10th try failed: dead-lettered.
- Dead-lettered and resubmitted:
- 10. Azure Monitor sends to Operations: Alert: dead-lettered > 0.
- 11. Operations calls Dead-letter queue: Peek the dead-lettered message.
- 12. Dead-letter queue replies to Operations: OrderPlaced, reason: MaxDeliveryCountExceeded.
- 13. Operations sends to order-events: Fix the cause, resubmit with a new MessageId.
- 14. order-events sends to Invoicing: OrderPlaced again.
- 15. Invoicing replies to order-events: Complete, invoiced already.
- 16. order-events sends to Warehouse: OrderPlaced again.
- 17. Warehouse replies to order-events: Complete, stock reserved.

Key
- Arrow with a filled head: Call
- Dashed arrow: Reply
- Arrow with an open head: Async message
