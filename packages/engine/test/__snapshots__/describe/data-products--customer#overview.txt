Customer data products

Data products, overview: From operational systems to fit-for-purpose products. 8 elements in 3 groups, with 8 connections.

Groups and elements
- Sales (Domain · Sales engineering)
  - Webshop (Source · Application)
  - Orders (Source-aligned)
- Customer (Domain · CRM team)
  - Customer 360 (Aggregate)
  - Salesforce (Source · SaaS)
  - Customers (Source-aligned)
- Sales dashboard (Dashboard)
- Marketing (Domain)
  - Churn risk (Consumer-aligned)
- Campaign tool (Application)

Connections
- Webshop connects to Orders (CDC).
- Orders connects to Customer 360 and connects to Sales dashboard.
- Customer 360 connects to Sales dashboard and connects to Churn risk.
- Churn risk connects to Campaign tool.
- Salesforce connects to Customers (daily).
- Customers connects to Customer 360.

Walkthrough
1. Four data products between two operational systems and the people who use the data.
2. Published at the source: Orders and customers come from the teams that run the systems, each under a contract.
3. One customer, one profile: Customer 360 joins both. Nobody downstream reads the operational systems again.
4. Where a churn score comes from: Marketing builds on the profile and serves scores to the campaign tool.
  - Churn risk: Owned by Marketing
