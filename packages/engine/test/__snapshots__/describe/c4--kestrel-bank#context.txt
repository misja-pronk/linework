Kestrel Online — system context

C4 model: The people and systems it works with. 5 elements, with 4 connections.

Elements
- Personal customer (Person): Holds accounts at Kestrel Bank
- Kestrel Online (Software System): Lets customers check balances, move money and manage their cards
- Core ledger (Software System): Holds every account and booking
- Postbird (Software System, external): Sends transactional email
- Push gateway (Software System, external): Delivers notifications to iOS and Android

Connections
- Personal customer connects to Kestrel Online (Banks online [HTTPS], Banks on the phone).
- Kestrel Online connects to Core ledger (Reads and books [ISO 20022 over MQ]), connects to Postbird (Sends email [SMTP]) and connects to Push gateway (Sends pushes [HTTPS]).

Key
- Indigo icon: Person
- Blue icon: Software system
- Icon, greyed: External system
- Solid line: Relationship
