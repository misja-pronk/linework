Walkthrough
1. Domains publish products: Each domain publishes from its own prod catalog; one platform team runs the lakehouse.
2. Read once from the source: Lakeflow Connect reads the ERP, web shop, Salesforce and the suppliers' SFTP server.
3. Products built on products: Sell-through and the forecast read other domains' gold tables, never their sources.
4. Used inside Northwind: Power BI and Customer 360 query gold via a SQL warehouse; replenishment calls an endpoint.
5. Shared with Contoso: Sell-through is in an OpenSharing share too; Contoso reads it in its own Databricks account.
6. Under a contract: Most products publish under an ODCS data contract; the detailed views show each one.
