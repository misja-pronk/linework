Connections
- dc01.northwind.local connects to dnspr-hub (Private network).
- dnspr-hub connects to privatelink.database.windows.net (Management), connects to privatelink.blob.core.windows.net (Management) and connects to dnsfrs-hub (Management).
- vmss-claims connects to dnspr-hub (Management: DNS), connects to pe-sql-claims (Private network) and connects to pe-st-claims (Private network).
- pe-sql-claims connects to sql-claims-prd (Private network: TDS 1433).
- pe-st-claims connects to stclaimsprd (Private network: HTTPS 443).

Walkthrough
1. Two PaaS services show the pattern, each reachable only through a private endpoint in the spoke.
2. The app asks for a name: sql-claims-prd.database.windows.net is a CNAME to the privatelink name; the resolver answers from the private zone with 10.1.9.4.
3. And connects privately: The connection goes to the endpoint's private IP in snet-pe, never over the internet.
4. From the office: The office DNS forwards privatelink.* to the resolver's inbound endpoint, so on-premises gets the same private IPs.
5. Every service the same way: Key Vault, Service Bus, Cosmos DB… each gets its own endpoint in snet-pe and its own privatelink zone, linked to the hub once.
