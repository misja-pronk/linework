Hub and spoke

Azure infrastructure diagram: Landing zone with shared connectivity. 11 elements in 7 groups, with 12 connections.

Groups and elements
- Head office (On-premises)
  - Employees (Users)
  - Office router (VPN Gateway)
- Connectivity (Subscription)
  - vnet-hub · 10.0.0.0/16 (Virtual network)
    - vgw-hub (VPN Gateway)
    - afw-hub (Firewall)
    - privatelink zones (DNS zone)
  - Central logs (Log Analytics)
- Online shop (Subscription)
  - vnet-shop · 10.1.0.0/16 (Virtual network)
    - snet-web (Subnet)
      - Shop (App Service)
      - agw-shop (Application Gateway)
    - snet-data (Subnet)
      - pe-sql (Private endpoint)
      - Shop DB (SQL Database)
- Internet

Connections
- Employees connect to Office router.
- Office router connects to vgw-hub (Private network: IPsec).
- vgw-hub connects to afw-hub (Private network).
- afw-hub connects to Shop (Private network: peering) and connects to Central logs (Management: diagnostics).
- Shop connects to Central logs (Management: diagnostics), connects to pe-sql (Private network: SQL) and connects to privatelink zones (resolve).
- pe-sql connects to Shop DB (Private network).
- Internet sends traffic to agw-shop over HTTPS.
- agw-shop sends traffic to Shop and connects to Central logs (Management: diagnostics).

Key
- Solid line with ticks: Connection
- Dotted line: Connection (dotted)
- Solid sky line: Public traffic
- Dashed teal line: Private network
- Badge: Management badge
