Connections
- Edge router connects to vpng-hub (Private network: IPsec).
- vpng-hub connects to afw-hub (Private network: UDR).
- afw-hub connects to vmss-erp (Private network: peering), connects to vmss-intranet (Private network: peering) and sends traffic to Internet (SNAT).
- bas-hub connects to vmss-erp (Management: RDP).

Walkthrough
1. The hub holds the shared network services; each workload gets its own spoke.
2. From the office: The office reaches the spokes over the VPN. The gateway hands the traffic to the firewall, not straight to the peering.
3. Out to the internet: The route table on each spoke subnet points 0.0.0.0/0 at the firewall's private IP, 10.0.0.68.
4. Spoke to spoke: Spokes are not peered to each other. ERP and intranet only meet through the firewall rules.
5. Admin access: No public IPs on VMs. Admins come in through Bastion, over the peering.

Layers
Ways to look at this drawing by tag.
- Routing: Spoke subnets send 0.0.0.0/0 to the firewall; the gateway sends the spoke ranges there too. (tags routing; 6 elements).
- Security: One way out to the internet, admin access only through Bastion. (tags security; 4 elements).
