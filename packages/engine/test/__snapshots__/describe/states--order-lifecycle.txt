Order lifecycle

States diagram: The states an order can be in, and what moves it on. 9 states in 1 group, with 10 transitions.

Groups and elements
- Start
- Awaiting payment: entry / reserve stock
- Fulfilment
  - Start of Fulfilment
  - Picking: do / collect the items
  - Packed: entry / print the label
- Cancelled: entry / release stock
- End
- Shipped
- Delivered

Main path
1. Start
2. Awaiting payment: order placed
3. Picking: payment received
4. Cancelled: customer cancels
5. End

Transitions
- Start moves to Awaiting payment on order placed.
- Awaiting payment moves to Fulfilment on payment received and moves to Cancelled on 30 min without payment.
- Fulfilment moves to Cancelled on customer cancels.
- Cancelled moves to End.
- Start of Fulfilment moves to Picking.
- Picking moves to Packed on all items scanned.
- Packed moves to Shipped on handed to carrier.
- Shipped moves to Delivered on signed for.
- Delivered moves to End.
