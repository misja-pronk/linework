Walkthrough
1. Scopes: Management group, subscription, resource group, resource. A role assigned on a scope is inherited by everything below it.
2. Standing access: Read-only at the top for security and finance. Developers can change dev and read production.
3. Production through PIM: Developers are eligible for Contributor on rg-shop-prod. They activate it for up to 8 hours, with MFA and a reason, and it ends by itself.
  - e6: MFA and a reason
4. Operations, least privilege: Operations gets a custom role that can restart the apps and nothing more, and can activate secret management on the vault when needed.
5. No standing Owner: Platform admins are eligible for Owner on the management group. Activation needs another admin's approval, and every activation is in the audit log.
  - sg-azure-platform-admins: A second admin approves
