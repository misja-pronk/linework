Serverless compute

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind lakehouse · warehouses, jobs and Model Serving reach the accounts through the NCC's endpoints. 6 elements in 1 group, with 5 connections.

Groups and elements
- Serverless compute plane (Run by Databricks)
  - SQL warehouses and jobs (Serverless · dbw-lakehouse-prod)
  - ncc-lakehouse-prod (Attached to dbw-lakehouse-prod)
  - demand-forecast (Model Serving endpoint)
- stordersprod (ADLS Gen2 · one of six)
- stsupplyprod (ADLS Gen2 · model artifacts)
- stsalesprod (ADLS Gen2 · sell_through)

Connections
- SQL warehouses and jobs connects to ncc-lakehouse-prod (Private network).
- ncc-lakehouse-prod connects to stordersprod (Private network: dfs), connects to stsupplyprod (Private network: blob · dfs) and connects to stsalesprod (Private network: dfs).
- demand-forecast connects to ncc-lakehouse-prod (Private network).

Walkthrough
1. Serverless compute: Serverless runs in Databricks' account; ncc-lakehouse-prod gives it private endpoints to Northwind's.
2. Reading data: A dfs rule per domain account, approved on the account; the account allows no public network access.
3. Model Serving: Serving downloads model artifacts over blob, so stsupplyprod has a blob rule as well as dfs.

Key
- Dashed teal line: Private network
