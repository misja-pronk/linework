A new developer's first two weeks

Journey map: From the first day to the first incident, as it goes today. Dana Okafor's journey through 4 phases and 8 steps.

Persona: Dana Okafor
- Role: Backend developer, joined the Payments team
- Goal: Ship something real in the first week
- Says: “I want to be useful, not a tourist in the codebase.”

By phase
- First day
  - Gets a laptop and a welcome session (feels welcome, 4 of 5)
    - Says: “Everyone is friendly. Where do I start?”
    - Through Laptop and Slack
    - With People lead
    - Works well: A buddy is assigned on day one
  - Requests access to code and cloud (feels waiting, 2 of 5)
    - Thinks: “Seven forms, and I do not know half of these names.”
    - Through Service desk portal and Email
    - With Service desk
    - Pain: One request per system
    - Pain: Approval takes two to three days
    - Idea: One access bundle per team role
    - Measured: 7 requests, 3 days
- First commit
  - Sets up the local environment (feels lost, 1 of 5)
    - Thinks: “The wiki says Node 16. The build wants 20.”
    - Through Wiki and Terminal
    - Pain: Setup guide is out of date
    - Pain: Secrets are passed around in chat
    - Idea: Dev container that works on the first try
    - Measured: 1.5 days
  - Opens a first pull request (feels proud, 4 of 5)
    - Says: “Reviewed within the hour. That felt good.”
    - Through GitHub and CI pipeline
    - With Buddy
    - Works well: Small starter issues are ready
    - Works well: Fast, kind reviews
- First deploy
  - Ships the change through the pipeline (feels unsure, 3 of 5)
    - Thinks: “Is it live now? Which stage is this?”
    - Through CI pipeline and Deploy dashboard
    - Pain: Nobody explains the release stages
    - Idea: A pipeline view that shows where a change is
    - Measured: 40 min per run
  - Sees the change live in production (feels thrilled, 5 of 5)
    - Says: “My code handles real payments!”
    - Through Grafana
    - With Team
    - Works well: The team celebrates first deploys
- First incident
  - Shadows an on-call shift (feels anxious, 2 of 5)
    - Thinks: “I would not know where to look on my own.”
    - Through PagerDuty and Runbook
    - With On-call engineer
    - Pain: Runbooks assume years of context
    - Pain: Dashboards have no owner
    - Idea: Game day in the first month
  - Fixes a small incident with help (feels capable, 4 of 5)
    - Says: “Now I see how the pieces fit.”
    - Through Grafana and Slack
    - With On-call engineer
    - Works well: Blameless review within two days
    - Measured: 35 min to recover

Feeling
Lowest at “Sets up the local environment” (1 of 5); highest at “Sees the change live in production” (5 of 5).

Metrics
- Time to first commit: 4 days
- Time to first deploy: 9 days
- Access requests: 7
