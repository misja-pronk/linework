Northwind finance access packages

Roles, groups and access packages diagram: Who may request what, who approves, and for how long. 14 elements in 2 groups, with 14 connections.

  - Finance basics (Access package)
  - ERP key user (Access package)

- Northwind ERP (Enterprise application)
- Fabrikam (Connected organisation · auditors)
- External partners (Catalog)

Connections
- Finance staff connects to Assigned automatically and connects to Manager → Finance controller approves.
- Assigned automatically connects to Finance basics.
- Manager → Finance controller approves connects to ERP key user.
- Finance basics connects to Finance team, connects to Finance team site (Member) and connects to Northwind ERP (Finance user).
- ERP key user connects to Northwind ERP (Key user).
- Fabrikam connects to Finance controller approves.
- Finance controller approves connects to Year-end audit.
- Year-end audit connects to Finance team site (Visitor) and connects to Finance reports (Viewer).
- Emma de Wit connects to Finance basics.
- Sam Okafor connects to Year-end audit.

Walkthrough
1. Catalogs: Finance owns its own catalog. Partner access lives in a separate catalog, with its own owners.
2. Basics, automatically: Everyone in Finance gets the team, the site and the ERP without asking, and loses them when they leave Finance.
3. More rights, two approvals: Key users are approved by their manager and then by the controllers. The access ends after 180 days unless a review keeps it.
4. Guests: Fabrikam's auditors request access themselves. The controllers approve, it is read-only, and it ends after 90 days.
  - Sam Okafor: Guest removed when the last package ends

Key
- Solid green line with ticks: Holds package
- Dashed line: Can request
- Solid violet line: Package grants
