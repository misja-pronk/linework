One order, reported

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind lakehouse · the order joined into sell-through and read in Power BI. 7 elements in 4 groups, with 6 connections.

Groups and elements
- sales-pipeline (Nightly)
- sales_prod (Catalog)
  - gold (Schema)
    - sell_through (Table)
- orders_prod (Catalog)
  - gold (Schema)
- bi-warehouse (Serverless SQL warehouse)
- Power BI (Fabric capacity F64): Reports on gold through the SQL warehouse
- Business analysts (Power BI · Genie): Read gold data in Power BI and Genie
- customers_prod (Catalog)
  - gold (Schema)

Connections
- sales-pipeline connects to sales_prod (Management: refreshes).
- gold (Schema) sends data to gold (Schema) (orders).
- gold (Schema) sends data to bi-warehouse.
- bi-warehouse sends data to Power BI (DirectQuery).
- Power BI sends data to Business analysts (Reports).
- gold (Schema) sends data to gold (Schema) (customers).

Walkthrough
1. Sell-through: At night sales-pipeline joins the order with its customer into sales_prod.gold.sell_through, another domain's gold.
2. In Power BI: In the morning the order is in the sales report; Power BI queries gold through bi-warehouse (DirectQuery).

Key
- Solid violet line with ticks: Data flow
- Dotted line with ticks: Management
