Platform cluster

Kubernetes diagram: Shared ingress, certificates and observability for the app teams. 15 elements in 6 groups, with 14 connections.

Groups and elements
- Internet
- platform-prod (Cluster)
  - ingress-nginx (Namespace)
    - ingress-nginx (LoadBalancer · ×3)
  - shop (Namespace)
    - shop (Ingress · shop.acme.io)
    - storefront (Deployment · ×3)
    - orders (Deployment · ×2)
    - orders-db (StatefulSet)
  - billing (Namespace)
    - billing (Ingress · pay.acme.io)
    - billing-api (Deployment · ×2)
    - billing-db (StatefulSet)
    - invoices (CronJob · 0 2 * * *)
  - observability (Namespace)
    - grafana (Deployment)
    - prometheus (StatefulSet · ×2)
    - loki (StatefulSet)
    - promtail (DaemonSet)
  - cert-manager (Namespace)
    - cert-manager (Deployment · ×2 · ACME)

Connections
- Internet sends traffic to ingress-nginx (LoadBalancer) over HTTPS.
- ingress-nginx (LoadBalancer) sends traffic to shop (Ingress) and sends traffic to billing (Ingress).
- shop (Ingress) sends traffic to storefront (/), sends traffic to orders (/api) and mounts shop-tls.
- billing (Ingress) sends traffic to billing-api.
- orders sends traffic to orders-db.
- billing-api sends traffic to billing-db.
- invoices sends traffic to billing-db.
- grafana sends traffic to prometheus and sends traffic to loki.
- promtail sends traffic to loki (logs).
- cert-manager (Deployment) mounts letsencrypt-account.

- Solid blue line: Traffic
