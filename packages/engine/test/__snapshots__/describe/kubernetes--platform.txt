Connections
- Internet sends traffic to ingress-nginx (LoadBalancer) over HTTPS.
- ingress-nginx (LoadBalancer) sends traffic to controller.
- controller sends traffic to shop (Ingress) and sends traffic to billing (Ingress).
- shop (Ingress) sends traffic to storefront (/), sends traffic to orders (/api) and mounts shop-tls.
- billing (Ingress) sends traffic to billing-api.
- storefront sends traffic to orders.
- orders sends traffic to billing-api (charge) and sends traffic to orders-db.
- billing-api sends traffic to billing-db.
- cert-manager (Deployment) sends traffic to Let's Encrypt (ACME) and mounts letsencrypt-account.
- invoices sends traffic to billing-db.
- grafana sends traffic to prometheus and sends traffic to loki.
- promtail sends traffic to loki (logs).
