E-commerce context map

Context map: Bounded contexts and how they integrate. 7 elements in 2 groups, with 6 connections.

Groups and elements
- Sales (Domain)
  - Catalogue (Supporting subdomain)
  - Ordering (Core domain)
  - Pricing (Core domain)
- Fulfilment (Domain)
  - Shipping (Supporting subdomain)
  - Warehouse ERP (Legacy system)
- Billing (Generic subdomain)
- Payment provider (External system)

Connections
- Catalogue (upstream, open host service, published language) and Ordering (downstream): Customer/Supplier.
- Ordering (upstream) and Shipping (downstream): Customer/Supplier.
- Ordering (upstream) and Billing (downstream): Customer/Supplier.
- Pricing is linked with Ordering (Partnership).
- Warehouse ERP (upstream) and Shipping (downstream, anticorruption layer): stock levels.
- Payment provider (upstream) and Billing (downstream, conformist).
