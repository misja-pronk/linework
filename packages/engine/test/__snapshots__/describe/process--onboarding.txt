Onboard a new colleague

Process diagram: HR and IT prepare at the same time and the manager plans once the accounts exist, so day one starts with a laptop and a plan. 11 elements in 3 lanes, with 11 connections.

Lanes and steps
- HR (lane)
  - Contract signed (start event)
  - Prepare (parallel split or join)
  - Register in HR system
- IT (lane)
  - Order laptop
  - Create accounts (subprocess, mail, VPN and source control)
- Manager (lane)
  - All ready (parallel split or join)
  - First day (timer intermediate event)
  - Plan the first week
  - Welcome and hand over laptop
  - Onboarded (end event)
  - A buddy from the team is named in the plan (note)

Main path
1. Contract signed
2. Prepare
3. Register in HR system
4. All ready
5. First day
6. Welcome and hand over laptop
7. Onboarded

Connections
- Contract signed leads to Prepare.
- Prepare leads to Register in HR system and leads to Order laptop.
- Register in HR system leads to All ready.
- Order laptop leads to Create accounts.
- All ready leads to First day.
- Create accounts leads to Plan the first week (accounts ready).
- First day leads to Welcome and hand over laptop.
- Plan the first week leads to All ready.
- Welcome and hand over laptop leads to Onboarded.
- A buddy from the team is named in the plan is a note on Plan the first week.
