Inside the cluster

Architecture diagram: Multi-tenant SaaS on Kubernetes, its teams and their data. 10 elements in 4 groups, with 9 connections.

Groups and elements
- ALB (Load balancer)
- EKS cluster
  - Core team
    - Bookings (Service)
    - Availability search
  - Growth team
    - Billing (Service)
    - Notifier (Worker)
- Data tier (Zone)
  - Postgres (RDS)
  - Sessions (ElastiCache)
  - booking-events (Kafka)
- Stripe (Payments)
- SendGrid (Email)

Connections
- ALB calls Bookings and calls Availability search.
- Bookings sends data to Postgres, sends data to Sessions and sends BookingConfirmed to booking-events.
- booking-events sends messages to Billing and sends messages to Notifier.
- Billing calls Stripe (charge).
- Notifier calls SendGrid.

Layers
Ways to look at this drawing by tag.
- Phase 1: Guests book through the app; one service, one database. (tags phase-1; 4 elements).
- Phase 2: Search, billing and notifications join, around an event stream. (tags phase-2; 8 elements).
- Data stores (tags data; 3 elements).

Key
- Solid blue line: Request
- Dashed violet line: Async message
- Solid teal line with ticks: Data flow
