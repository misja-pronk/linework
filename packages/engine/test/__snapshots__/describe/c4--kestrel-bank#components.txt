Banking API — components

C4 model: Inside the Spring Boot service. 11 elements in 2 groups, with 11 connections.

Groups and elements
- Kestrel Online (Software System): Lets customers check balances, move money and manage their cards
  - Web banking (Container: Next.js): Serves online banking to the customer's browser
  - Banking API (Container: Spring Boot): Signs customers in and serves accounts and payments as JSON
    - Sign-in controller (Component: Spring MVC): Starts a session once the credentials check out
    - Accounts controller (Component: Spring MVC): Lists accounts, balances and recent transactions
    - Session service (Component: Spring Security): Verifies passwords and one-time codes
    - Ledger client (Component: Spring bean): Reads and books on the core ledger
    - Payments controller (Component: Spring MVC): Checks and submits transfers
    - Event publisher (Component: Spring Cloud Stream): Announces what happened to an account
  - Customer store (Container: PostgreSQL 16): Logins, devices, preferences and sessions
  - Mobile app (Container: Kotlin and Swift): Balances, transfers and cards on the phone
  - Account events (Container: Kafka): Payments and sign-ins as they happen
- Core ledger (Software System): Holds every account and books every transaction

Connections
- Web banking connects to Sign-in controller (Signs in [JSON/HTTPS]) and connects to Accounts controller (Gets accounts [JSON/HTTPS]).
- Sign-in controller connects to Session service (Checks credentials).
- Accounts controller connects to Ledger client (Gets balances).
- Session service connects to Customer store (Reads logins [JDBC]).
- Ledger client connects to Core ledger (Reads and books [ISO 20022 over MQ]).
- Mobile app connects to Sign-in controller (Signs in [JSON/HTTPS]) and connects to Payments controller (Submits transfers [JSON/HTTPS]).
- Payments controller connects to Ledger client (Books transfers) and connects to Event publisher (Reports payments).
- Event publisher sends messages to Account events (Publishes [Kafka]).
