Walkthrough
1. Scopes: Management group, subscription, resource group, resource. A role assigned on a scope is inherited by everything below it.
2. Standing access: Read-only at the top for security and finance. Developers can change dev and read production.
3. Production through PIM: Developers are eligible for Contributor on rg-shop-prod. They activate it for a few hours, with a reason, and it ends by itself.
  - sg-shop-developers: MFA and a reason, max 8 h
4. Operations, least privilege: Operations gets a custom role that can restart the apps and nothing more, and can activate secret management on the vault when needed.
5. No standing Owner: Platform admins are eligible for Owner on the management group. Activation needs approval, and every activation is logged and reviewed.
  - sg-azure-platform-admins: A second admin approves, max 4 h
