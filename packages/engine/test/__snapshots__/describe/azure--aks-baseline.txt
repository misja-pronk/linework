Connections
- agw-orders-prod connects to traefik (Private network).
- traefik connects to orders-api (Private network).
- orders-api signs in with id-orders-prod and connects to pep-kv-orders-prod (Private network: secrets).
- id-orders-prod is Key Vault Secrets User on kv-orders-prod.
- pep-kv-orders-prod connects to kv-orders-prod (Private network).
- aks-orders-prod connects to pep-cr-orders-prod (Private network: image pull) and connects to afw-hub-weu (Private network: egress · UDR).
- pep-cr-orders-prod connects to crordersprod (Private network).

Walkthrough
1. A private cluster: A private AKS cluster in its own spoke, with a subnet per role.
2. In from the internet: Customers reach the WAF's public IP over HTTPS; it forwards to the ingress controller on an internal load balancer.
  - agw-orders-prod: HTTPS from the internet: the only public entry
3. Secrets without passwords: The pod signs in as id-orders-prod through workload identity and reads Key Vault over a private endpoint.
4. Images: Nodes pull images from the registry over a private endpoint; the registry has public access off.
5. Out: The node subnet's route table sends all egress to the hub firewall, which allows only what is needed.

Layers
Ways to look at this drawing by tag.
- Ingress: One public entry, the WAF on Application Gateway; the ingress controller sits on an internal load balancer. (tags ingress; 3 elements).
- Egress: Nothing leaves the cluster except through the hub firewall, which allows only the FQDNs AKS and the apps need. (tags egress; 1 element).
- Identity: Pods sign in as id-orders-prod through workload identity; no credentials are stored in the cluster. (tags identity; 4 elements).
