AKS baseline

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind orders platform · private cluster, WAF in front, egress through the hub firewall, no credentials in the cluster. 9 elements in 7 groups, with 9 connections.

Groups and elements
- sub-orders-prd (Landing zone · Corp)
  - vnet-aks-weu (10.2.0.0/16)
    - snet-appgw (10.2.0.0/24)
      - agw-orders (WAF v2 · public IP)
    - snet-nodes (10.2.16.0/20)
      - aks-orders-prd (Private cluster)
        - ingress-nginx (Internal LB)
        - orders-api (3 pods)
    - snet-pe (10.2.1.0/24)
      - pe-kv (Private endpoint)
      - pe-acr (Private endpoint)
  - rg-orders-shared (Resource group)
    - id-orders (Workload identity)
    - kv-orders-prd (RBAC · CSI driver)
    - crordersprd (Premium · private)
- afw-hub (Hub · FQDN rules)

Connections
- agw-orders connects to ingress-nginx (Private network: HTTPS).
- ingress-nginx connects to orders-api (Private network).
- orders-api signs in with id-orders and connects to pe-kv (Private network: secrets).
- id-orders is Secrets User on kv-orders-prd.
- pe-kv connects to kv-orders-prd (Private network).
- aks-orders-prd connects to pe-acr (Private network: image pull) and connects to afw-hub (Private network: egress · UDR).
- pe-acr connects to crordersprd (Private network).

Walkthrough
1. A private AKS cluster in its own spoke, with a subnet per role.
2. In from the internet: Customers reach the WAF's public IP; it forwards to the ingress controller on an internal load balancer.
  - agw-orders: The only public entry
3. Secrets without passwords: The pod is federated to id-orders, which reads Key Vault over its private endpoint.
4. Images: Nodes pull images from the registry over a private endpoint; the registry has public access off.
5. Out: The node subnet's route table sends all egress to the hub firewall, which allows only what is needed.

Layers
Ways to look at this drawing by tag.
- Ingress: One public entry, the WAF on Application Gateway; the ingress controller sits on an internal load balancer. (tags ingress; 3 elements).
- Egress: Nothing leaves the cluster except through the hub firewall, which allows only the FQDNs AKS and the apps need. (tags egress; 1 element).
- Identity: Pods sign in as id-orders through workload identity; no credentials are stored in the cluster. (tags identity; 4 elements).
