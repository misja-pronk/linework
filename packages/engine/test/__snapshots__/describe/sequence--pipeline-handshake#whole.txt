Nightly export handshake

Sequence, whole sequence: A partner drops a file; we load it in parallel and confirm or reject the batch. 5 participants and 16 messages.

Participants
- Partner SFTP (External system)
- Data platform
  - Ingest trigger (Function)
  - Orchestrator (Worker)
  - Raw zone (Storage)
  - Warehouse (Database)

Message flow
In order, top to bottom.
- Arrival:
- 1. Partner SFTP sends to Ingest trigger: orders_2026-11-09.csv + .done marker.
- 2. Ingest trigger calls Raw zone: Copy file and checksum.
- 3. Raw zone replies to Ingest trigger: stored.
- 4. Ingest trigger sends to Orchestrator: BatchArrived(batch 8812).
- Load:
- 5. Orchestrator calls Raw zone: Read manifest.
- 6. Raw zone replies to Orchestrator: 3 files, 1.2 GB.
- In parallel: orders
  - 7. Orchestrator calls Warehouse: COPY orders.
  - 8. Warehouse replies to Orchestrator: 1,204,331 rows.
- And: order_lines
  - 9. Orchestrator calls Warehouse: COPY order_lines.
  - 10. Warehouse replies to Orchestrator: 4,881,020 rows.
- Check and confirm:
- 11. Orchestrator calls Warehouse: Run quality checks.
- 12. Warehouse replies to Orchestrator: Results.
- Alternative: All checks pass
  - 13. Orchestrator calls Warehouse: Swap staging into production.
  - 14. Orchestrator sends to Partner SFTP: ACK batch 8812.
- Else: Row counts differ from the manifest
  - 15. Orchestrator calls Raw zone: Move batch to quarantine.
  - 16. Orchestrator sends to Partner SFTP: NACK batch 8812, reason.
- See Retry and back-off policy (over Partner SFTP and Orchestrator)

Key
- Arrow with a filled head: Call
- Dashed arrow: Reply
- Arrow with an open head: Async message
