Northwind lakehouse

Architecture diagram: Sources, the lakehouse and Unity Catalog, and who uses the data. 11 elements, with 10 connections.

Elements
- Business analysts (Users): Read gold data in Power BI and Genie
- Power BI (Fabric capacity F64): Reports on gold through the SQL warehouse
- Northwind lakehouse (System): Pipelines, jobs and serving, deployed from Git to dev, test and prod
- uc-weu (Unity Catalog · West Europe): No metastore storage; every catalog has its own managed location
- Contoso Retail (External system): Retail partner; reads sell-through in its own Databricks account
- Sales reps (Users): Look up customers in the Customer 360 app
- ERP (SQL Server · on-premises): Orders and invoices and stock
- sqldb-webshop-prod (Azure SQL Database): Web shop orders and accounts
- evhns-webshop-prod (Event Hubs · clickstream): Clickstream events of the web shop
- Salesforce (Sales Cloud): Accounts and opportunities
- Replenishment (Service): Orders stock from suppliers on the demand forecast

Connections
- Business analysts connect to Power BI (Reports).
- Power BI sends data to Northwind lakehouse (DirectQuery).
- Northwind lakehouse sends data to uc-weu (Registers, Serves, Reads).
- uc-weu sends data to Contoso Retail (OpenSharing).
- Sales reps connect to Northwind lakehouse (Looks up customers).
- ERP sends data to Northwind lakehouse (CDC [SQL Server]).
- sqldb-webshop-prod sends data to Northwind lakehouse (CDC).
- evhns-webshop-prod sends data to Northwind lakehouse (Kafka).
- Salesforce sends data to Northwind lakehouse (API).
- Replenishment connects to Northwind lakehouse (Scores [REST]).

Key
- Solid line: Connection
- Solid teal line with ticks: Data flow
