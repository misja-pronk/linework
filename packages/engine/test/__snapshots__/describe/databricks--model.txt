Northwind lakehouse

Architecture diagram: Where the data comes from, where it lives and who uses it. 12 elements, with 11 connections.

Elements
- ERP (SQL Server · on-premises): Orders and invoices and stock
- Northwind lakehouse (System): Pipelines, jobs and serving, deployed from Git to dev, test and prod
- uc-weu (Unity Catalog · West Europe): No metastore storage; every catalog has its own managed location
- Replenishment (Supply planning app): Orders stock from suppliers on the demand forecast
- Power BI (Fabric capacity F64): Reports on gold through the SQL warehouse
- Sales reps (Customer 360 app): Look up customers in the Customer 360 app
- Contoso Retail (Retail partner · own Databricks account): Retail partner; reads sell-through in its own Databricks account
- Business analysts (Power BI · Genie): Read gold data in Power BI and Genie
- sqldb-webshop-prod (Azure SQL Database): Web shop orders and accounts
- evhns-webshop-prod (Event Hubs · clickstream): Clickstream events of the web shop
- Salesforce (Sales Cloud): Accounts and opportunities
- Supplier SFTP (SFTP server · CSV files): Stock and lead times that suppliers upload

Connections
- ERP sends data to Northwind lakehouse (CDC [SQL Server]).
- Northwind lakehouse sends data to uc-weu, sends data to Replenishment (Forecast [REST]), sends data to Power BI (DirectQuery) and sends data to Sales reps (Customer 360).
- uc-weu sends data to Contoso Retail (OpenSharing).
- Power BI sends data to Business analysts (Reports).
- sqldb-webshop-prod sends data to Northwind lakehouse (CDC).
- evhns-webshop-prod sends data to Northwind lakehouse (Kafka).
- Salesforce sends data to Northwind lakehouse (API).
- Supplier SFTP sends data to uc-weu over SFTP · Auto Loader.
