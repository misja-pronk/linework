Requesting access to a production system

Journey map, storyboard: Storyboard. Sam de Vries's journey through 5 phases and 6 steps.

Persona: Sam de Vries
- Role: Engineer who needs production access for an on-call shift
- Goal: Be able to act when the pager goes off tonight
- Says: “I do not mind rules. I mind not knowing where my request is.”

By phase
- Need
  - Finds out which access is needed (feels guessing, 2 of 5)
    - Thinks: “Which of these forty groups do I need?”
    - Through Wiki and Slack
    - With Colleague
    - Pain: Group names say nothing about what they allow
- Request
  - Files a ticket with a free-text reason (feels resigned, 2 of 5)
    - Thinks: “I hope I picked the right category.”
    - Through Service desk portal
    - Pain: Free-text form, often sent back
    - Pain: No overview of what is already requested
- Approve
  - Waits for the manager to approve (feels blocked, 1 of 5)
    - Thinks: “The shift starts tonight and nobody has answered.”
    - Through Email
    - With Manager
    - Pain: Approver is on leave, no delegate
    - Pain: No status anywhere
    - Measured: 3 days
  - Chases the system owner for a second approval (feels frustrated, 1 of 5)
    - Says: “Can you please look at ticket 48213?”
    - Through Email and Slack
    - With System owner
    - Pain: Two approvals in sequence, by hand
    - Measured: 2 days
- Use
  - Gets permanent access from the service desk (feels relieved, 3 of 5)
    - Says: “It works. I will keep this forever, just in case.”
    - Through Azure portal
    - With Service desk
    - Pain: Standing access that nobody takes away
- Review
  - Is asked a year later why the access exists (feels caught out, 2 of 5)
    - Thinks: “I do not remember. Probably that one shift.”
    - Through Email
    - With Auditor
    - Pain: Reviews are yearly spreadsheets

Feeling
Lowest at “Waits for the manager to approve” (1 of 5); highest at “Gets permanent access from the service desk” (3 of 5).

Metrics
- Lead time: 5 days
- Hand-offs: 4
- Standing access: Yes
- Lead time: 15 minutes
- Hand-offs: 1
- Standing access: No
