Connections
- Core router connects to Direct Connect (VPN / interconnect: BGP).
- vpc-prod connects to Transit gateway (Private network: attachment).
- Transit gateway connects to Direct Connect (VPN / interconnect) and connects to Inspection (Private network: 0.0.0.0/0).
- Inspection connects to Central egress (Private network).
- Central egress sends traffic to Internet.
- vpc-dev connects to Transit gateway (Private network).
