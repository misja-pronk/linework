Harbour Bank — system landscape

C4 model. 8 elements in 1 group, with 9 connections.

Groups and elements
- Retail customer (Person): Banks online and on the phone
- Harbour Bank (Enterprise)
  - Harbour Online (Software System): Balances, transfers and cards for retail customers
  - Core ledger (Software System): Holds every account and books every transaction
  - Payments hub (Software System): Checks, routes and settles every payment
  - Data platform (Software System): Reporting and analytics on bank data
  - Branch advisor (Person): Helps customers in the branch
  - Advisor desk (Software System): Customer records and cases for branch staff
- SWIFT network (Software System, external): Interbank payments

Connections
- Retail customer connects to Harbour Online (Banks online [HTTPS], Banks on the phone).
- Harbour Online connects to Core ledger (Reads balances) and connects to Payments hub (Submits payments [gRPC]).
- Core ledger connects to Data platform (Nightly extract).
- Payments hub connects to Core ledger (Books), sends messages to Data platform (Streams) and connects to SWIFT network (Sends interbank payments [ISO 20022]).
- Branch advisor connects to Advisor desk (Looks up customers).
- Advisor desk connects to Core ledger (Reads accounts).

Key
- Indigo icon: Person
- Blue icon: Software system
- Icon, greyed: External system
