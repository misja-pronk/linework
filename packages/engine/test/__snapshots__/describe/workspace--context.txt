Harbour Online — system context

C4 model. 4 elements, with 4 connections.

Elements
- Retail customer (Person): Banks online and on the phone
- Harbour Online (Software System): Balances, transfers and cards for retail customers
- Core ledger (Software System): Holds every account and books every transaction
- Payments hub (Software System): Checks, routes and settles every payment

Connections
- Retail customer connects to Harbour Online (Banks online [HTTPS], Banks on the phone).
- Harbour Online connects to Core ledger (Reads balances) and connects to Payments hub (Submits payments [gRPC]).
- Payments hub connects to Core ledger (Books).

Key
- Indigo icon: Person
- Blue icon: Software system
- Solid line: Relationship
