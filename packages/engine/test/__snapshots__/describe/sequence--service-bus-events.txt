Events through Service Bus

Sequence diagram: Northwind orders: one event, two subscribers, each retried on its own, and what fails for good waits in the dead-letter queue. 6 participants and 10 messages.

Participants
- Orders API (App Service)
- Service Bus sb-northwind-prod
  - order-events (topic)
  - Dead-letter queue (per subscription)
- Invoicing (Azure Function)
- Warehouse (Container App)
- Operations

Message flow
In order, top to bottom.
- 1. Orders API sends to order-events: OrderPlaced, MessageId = order id.
- In parallel: Subscription invoicing
  - 2. order-events sends to Invoicing: OrderPlaced (peek-lock).
  - 3. Invoicing does: Create invoice, unless MessageId was seen.
  - 4. Invoicing replies to order-events: Complete.
- And: Subscription warehouse
  - 5. order-events sends to Warehouse: OrderPlaced (peek-lock).
  - 6. Warehouse replies to order-events: Abandon, stock service down.
  - 7. order-events sends to Warehouse: Redelivered, DeliveryCount 2 to 10.
  - 8. order-events sends to Dead-letter queue: After the 10th try, to the dead-letter queue.
- 9. Dead-letter queue sends to Operations: Alert: dead-lettered messages > 0.
- 10. Operations calls Dead-letter queue: Fix the cause, resubmit.

Key
- Arrow with a filled head: Call
- Dashed arrow: Reply
- Arrow with an open head: Async message
