Order to cash

Process diagram: A customer order from the moment it arrives until the money is booked. 14 elements in 3 lanes, with 15 connections.

Lanes and steps
- Sales (lane)
  - Order received (start event)
  - Confirm order
  - Ask for prepayment
- Finance (lane)
  - Check credit
  - Credit ok? (decision decision)
  - Send invoice
  - 30 days (timer intermediate event)
  - Invoice (document)
  - Paid? (decision decision)
  - Book payment
  - Send reminder
  - Order closed (end event)
- Warehouse (lane)
  - Pick and pack
  - Ship goods

Main path
1. Order received
2. Check credit
3. Credit ok?
4. Confirm order: Credit ok? yes
5. Pick and pack
6. Ship goods
7. Send invoice
8. 30 days
9. Paid?
10. Book payment: Paid? yes
11. Order closed

Connections
- Order received leads to Check credit.
- Check credit leads to Credit ok?
- Credit ok? leads to Confirm order (yes) and leads to Ask for prepayment (no).
- Confirm order leads to Pick and pack.
- Ask for prepayment leads to Confirm order.
- Pick and pack leads to Ship goods.
- Ship goods leads to Send invoice.
- Send invoice leads to 30 days and leads to Invoice.
- 30 days leads to Paid?
- Paid? leads to Book payment (yes) and leads to Send reminder (no).
- Book payment leads to Order closed.
- Send reminder leads to 30 days.

Key
- Solid line, thick: Happy path
- Solid line: Other paths
