Order to cash

Process diagram: A customer order from the moment it arrives until the money is booked. 13 elements in 3 lanes, with 14 connections.

Lanes and steps
- Sales (lane)
  - Order received (start event)
  - Confirm order
  - Ask for prepayment
- Warehouse (lane)
  - Pick, pack and ship
- Finance (lane)
  - Check credit
  - Credit ok? (decision)
  - Send invoice
  - 30 days (timer intermediate event)
  - Invoice (document)
  - Paid? (decision)
  - Book payment
  - Send reminder
  - Closed (end event)

Main path
1. Order received
2. Check credit
3. Credit ok?
4. Confirm order (Credit ok?: yes)
5. Pick, pack and ship
6. Send invoice
7. 30 days
8. Paid?
9. Book payment (Paid?: yes)
10. Closed

Connections
- Order received leads to Check credit.
- Check credit leads to Credit ok?
- Credit ok? leads to Confirm order (yes) and leads to Ask for prepayment (no).
- Confirm order leads to Pick, pack and ship.
- Ask for prepayment leads to Confirm order.
- Pick, pack and ship leads to Send invoice.
- Send invoice leads to 30 days and leads to Invoice.
- 30 days leads to Paid?
- Paid? leads to Book payment (yes) and leads to Send reminder (no).
- Book payment leads to Closed.
- Send reminder leads to 30 days.

Key
- Solid line, thick: Happy path
- Solid line: Other paths
- Dotted line: Document link
