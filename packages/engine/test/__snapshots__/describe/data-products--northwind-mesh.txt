- Revenue connects to CFO dashboard.

- Customers connects to Revenue and connects to Sales dashboard.
- Warehouse management connects to Stock levels (hourly).
- Stock levels connects to Demand forecast.
- General ledger connects to Revenue (nightly).

Walkthrough
1. Domains own their data: Sales, Supply chain and Finance each publish data products, and each product has a team that answers for it.
2. Source-aligned products: Each operational system is read once, by the team that knows it, and published under a contract.
3. Products built on products: Demand forecast and Revenue read other products through their output ports, never the systems behind them.
4. Served from a self-serve platform: Dashboards and applications read the products too. One platform team gives every domain the same storage, pipelines, catalogue and access, so a new product takes days, not months.
5. Federated governance: Orders, Customers and Revenue publish under a data contract. The domains agree on the rules every contract follows, such as classification, PII and freshness, and the platform checks them automatically. The detailed views show each contract.
