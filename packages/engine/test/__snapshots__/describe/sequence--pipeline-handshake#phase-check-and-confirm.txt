Nightly export handshake

Sequence, check and confirm: Check and confirm. 4 participants and 6 messages.

Participants
- Partner SFTP (External system)
- Data platform
  - Orchestrator (Worker)
  - Raw zone (Storage)
  - Warehouse (Database)

Message flow
In order, top to bottom.
- 1. Orchestrator calls Warehouse: Run quality checks.
- 2. Warehouse replies to Orchestrator: Results.
- Alternative: All checks pass
  - 3. Orchestrator calls Warehouse: Swap staging into production.
  - 4. Orchestrator sends to Partner SFTP: ACK batch 8812.
- Else: Row counts differ from the manifest
  - 5. Orchestrator calls Raw zone: Move batch to quarantine.
  - 6. Orchestrator sends to Partner SFTP: NACK batch 8812, reason.
- See Retry and back-off policy (over Partner SFTP and Orchestrator)

Key
- Arrow with a filled head: Call
- Dashed arrow: Reply
- Arrow with an open head: Async message
