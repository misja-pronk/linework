Hybrid connectivity

Network diagram: Northwind · ExpressRoute to the Azure hub, a site-to-site VPN over the internet as failover. 8 elements in 9 groups, with 8 connections.

Groups and elements
- Amsterdam DC (10.10.0.0/16)
  - rtr-ams (BGP AS 65010 · HA pair)
  - Servers (VLAN 100 · 10.10.100.0/24)
    - dc01 (AD DS + DNS · 10.10.100.10)
- ExpressRoute circuit (1 Gbps · private peering · Equinix AM)
  - MSEE (Microsoft edge · AS 12076 · HA pair)
- Azure West Europe (Cloud region)
  - vnet-hub-weu (10.0.0.0/22)
    - GatewaySubnet (10.0.0.0/27)
      - vpng-hub (VpnGw2AZ · active-active · AS 65515)
      - ergw-hub (ErGw1AZ)
    - AzureFirewallSubnet (10.0.0.64/26)
      - afw-hub (Azure Firewall Premium · 10.0.0.68)
  - vnet-erp-weu (10.1.0.0/22)
    - vmss-erp (rt-erp · propagation off)
  - vnet-intranet-weu (10.1.4.0/22)
    - vmss-intranet (rt-intranet · propagation off)

Connections
- rtr-ams is linked with dc01 (2×10G), connects to MSEE (Private circuit: eBGP · primary + secondary) and connects to vpng-hub (VPN tunnel: over the internet · BGP · prepend ×3).
- MSEE connects to ergw-hub (Private circuit: private peering).
- vpng-hub is linked with afw-hub.
- ergw-hub is linked with afw-hub.
- afw-hub connects to vmss-erp (Peering) and connects to vmss-intranet (Peering).

Walkthrough
1. Two ways from the data centre into the Azure hub, both through the hub firewall.
2. Normal operation: Traffic takes ExpressRoute. Azure prefers it over the VPN for the same prefix.
3. Circuit down: BGP withdraws the ExpressRoute routes within seconds. The VPN, until now the longer path, takes over.
  - MSEE: Down
4. Spokes stay put: Spokes send 0.0.0.0/0 to the firewall and never learn on-premises routes, so a failover does not touch them.

Layers
Ways to look at this drawing by tag.
- ExpressRoute: The normal path. Private, 1 Gbps, BGP over two links to the Microsoft edge. (tags expressroute; 7 elements).
- VPN failover: Only used when ExpressRoute is down. Same prefixes, longer AS path. (tags vpn; 6 elements).

Key
- Solid line: Ethernet
- Solid teal line with beads, thick: Private circuit
- Dashed teal line: VPN tunnel
- Dashed blue line with ticks: Peering
- Solid line, thick: Aggregated link (2× or more)
