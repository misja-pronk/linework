Hybrid connectivity

Network diagram: Northwind · ExpressRoute to the Azure hub, a site-to-site VPN over the internet as failover. 8 elements in 9 groups, with 8 connections.

Groups and elements
- Amsterdam DC (10.10.0.0/16)
  - Servers (VLAN 100 · 10.10.100.0/24)
    - dc01 (AD DS + DNS · 10.10.100.10)
  - rtr-ams (BGP AS 65010 · BFD · HA pair)
- ExpressRoute circuit (1 Gbps · Equinix AM)
  - MSEE (Microsoft edge · AS 12076 · HA pair)
- Azure West Europe (Cloud region)
  - vnet-hub-weu (10.0.0.0/22)
    - GatewaySubnet (10.0.0.0/27)
      - vpng-hub (VpnGw2AZ · AS 65515)
      - ergw-hub (ErGw1AZ)
    - AzureFirewallSubnet (10.0.0.64/26)
      - afw-hub (Azure Firewall Premium · 10.0.0.68)
  - vnet-erp-prod (10.1.0.0/22)
    - vmss-erp-prod (VM scale set)
  - vnet-intranet-prod (10.1.4.0/22)
    - vmss-intranet-prod (VM scale set)

Connections
- dc01 is linked with rtr-ams (2×10G).
- rtr-ams connects to MSEE (Private circuit: eBGP ×2) and connects to vpng-hub (VPN tunnel: IPsec · BGP).
- MSEE connects to ergw-hub (Private circuit: private peering).
- vpng-hub is linked with afw-hub.
- ergw-hub is linked with afw-hub.
- afw-hub connects to vmss-erp-prod (Peering) and connects to vmss-intranet-prod (Peering).

Walkthrough
1. Two ways in: Two ways from the data centre into the Azure hub, both through the hub firewall.
2. Normal operation: Traffic takes ExpressRoute. Azure prefers it over the VPN for the same prefix; so does the DC router.
3. Circuit down: BFD notices within a second and BGP withdraws the ExpressRoute routes. The VPN takes over.
  - MSEE: Down
4. Spokes stay put: Spoke route tables (gateway routes off) send 0.0.0.0/0 to the firewall, so a failover does not touch them.

Layers
Ways to look at this drawing by tag.
- ExpressRoute: The normal path. Private, 1 Gbps, BGP over two links to the Microsoft edge. (tags expressroute; 7 elements).
- VPN failover: Only used when ExpressRoute is down. Azure prefers ExpressRoute for the same prefix; the DC router prefers it by local preference. (tags vpn; 6 elements).

Key
- Solid line: Link
- Solid teal line with beads, thick: Private circuit
- Dashed teal line: VPN tunnel
- Dashed blue line with ticks: Peering
- Solid line, thick: Aggregated link (2× or more)
