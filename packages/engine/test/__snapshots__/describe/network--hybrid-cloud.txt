Hybrid cloud

Network diagram: Data centre to an Azure hub and spokes over ExpressRoute, with a VPN as backup. 11 elements in 8 groups, with 11 connections.

Groups and elements
- Amsterdam DC (10.0.0.0/16)
  - rtr-ams (Cisco ASR 1001-X · HA pair, a redundant pair)
  - fw-ams (Palo Alto PA-3420 · HA pair, a redundant pair)
  - core-ams (Nexus 93180 · HA vPC, a redundant pair)
  - Servers (VLAN 100 · 10.0.100.0/24)
    - esx-cluster (6 hosts)
    - dc-01 (DNS · 10.0.100.10)
- Azure West Europe (Cloud region)
  - vnet-hub (10.100.0.0/22)
    - GatewaySubnet (10.100.0.0/27)
      - ergw-hub (ExpressRoute ErGw2AZ)
      - vpngw-hub (VpnGw2AZ)
    - AzureFirewallSubnet (10.100.1.0/26)
      - afw-hub (Azure Firewall Premium · 10.100.1.4)
  - vnet-prod (10.101.0.0/16)
    - agw-prod (Application Gateway)
    - vmss-api (Server · 10.101.1.0/24)
  - vnet-dev (10.102.0.0/16)
    - vm-dev (Server · 10.102.1.0/24)

Connections
- rtr-ams connects to ergw-hub (Private circuit: ExpressRoute · 1G), connects to vpngw-hub (VPN tunnel: IPsec backup) and connects to fw-ams (Trunk: 2×10G).
- ergw-hub is linked with afw-hub.
- vpngw-hub is linked with afw-hub.
- fw-ams connects to core-ams (Trunk: 2×25G).
- afw-hub connects to agw-prod (Peering) and connects to vm-dev (Peering).
- core-ams is linked with esx-cluster (4×25G) and is linked with dc-01.
- agw-prod is linked with vmss-api.

Key
- Solid line: Ethernet
- Solid indigo line, double, thick: Trunk
- Solid teal line with beads, thick: Private circuit
- Dashed teal line: VPN tunnel
- Dashed blue line with ticks: Peering
- Solid line, thick: Aggregated link (2× or more)
