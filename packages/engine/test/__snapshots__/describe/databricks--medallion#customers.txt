Customers domain

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind lakehouse · accounts from Salesforce into customers_prod; the Customer 360 app reads them unmasked. 11 elements in 2 groups, with 10 connections.

Groups and elements
- mask_pii (ABAC column mask · governed tag pii): Masks every column tagged pii for everyone but the CRM team, the prod jobs and the Customer 360 app
- customers_prod (Catalog)
  - gold (Schema)
    - customers (Table)
  - bronze (Schema)
  - silver (Schema)
- Salesforce (Sales Cloud): Accounts and opportunities
- salesforce-ingest (Salesforce connector)
- sales_prod (Catalog)
- bi-warehouse (Serverless SQL warehouse)
- customer-360 (Databricks App): Runs as its own service principal; sees customers unmasked
- Sales reps (Customer 360 app): Look up customers in the Customer 360 app
- customers-pipeline (Lakeflow pipeline)

Connections
- mask_pii connects to customers (Management: masks pii columns).
- Salesforce sends data to salesforce-ingest (API).
- salesforce-ingest sends data to bronze.
- bronze sends data to silver (clean).
- silver sends data to gold (match).
- gold sends data to sales_prod (customers) and sends data to bi-warehouse.
- bi-warehouse sends data to customer-360 (SQL).
- customer-360 sends data to Sales reps (Customer 360).
- customers-pipeline connects to customers_prod (Management: refreshes).

Key
- Solid violet line with ticks: Data flow
- Dotted line with ticks: Management
