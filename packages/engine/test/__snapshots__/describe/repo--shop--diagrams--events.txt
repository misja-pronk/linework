Shop events

Architecture diagram: From the AsyncAPI files: who sends what to which channel. 5 elements in 2 groups, with 5 connections.

Groups and elements
- Orders service (v2.14.0)
- kafka.shop.internal (kafka)
  - shop.orders.v1
- rabbitmq.shop.internal (amqp)
  - emails
  - payments.results
- Payments service (v1.9.0)

Connections
- Orders service sends OrderPlaced, OrderCancelled to shop.orders.v1 and sends SendEmail to emails.
- shop.orders.v1 sends OrderPlaced to Payments service.
- Payments service sends PaymentSucceeded, PaymentFailed to payments.results.
- payments.results sends PaymentSucceeded, PaymentFailed to Orders service.

Key
- Dashed violet line: Async message
