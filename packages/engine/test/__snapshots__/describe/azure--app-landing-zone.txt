Application landing zone

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind customer portal · one subscription, a resource group per concern, plugged into the platform. 10 elements in 10 groups, with 13 connections.

Groups and elements
- sub-portal-prd (Landing zone · Corp)
  - rg-portal-app (Resource group)
    - app-portal (Managed identity)
    - func-portal-jobs (Managed identity)
  - rg-portal-network (Resource group)
    - vnet-portal-weu (10.1.12.0/22 · peered to the hub)
      - snet-app (10.1.12.0/24 · VNet integration)
        - rt-portal (0.0.0.0/0 → 10.0.0.68)
      - snet-pe (10.1.13.0/24)
        - pe-sql-portal (10.1.13.4)
        - pe-kv-portal (10.1.13.5)
  - rg-portal-monitor (Resource group)
    - appi-portal (Application Insights)
  - rg-portal-data (Resource group)
    - sqldb-portal (SQL Database)
    - kv-portal-prd (Key Vault)
- sub-management (Platform)
  - log-platform-weu (Central workspace)
- sub-connectivity (Platform)
  - afw-hub (Hub firewall · 10.0.0.68)

Connections
- app-portal connects to rt-portal (Private network: outbound), connects to pe-sql-portal (Private network), connects to pe-kv-portal (Private network), sends data to appi-portal and connects to log-platform-weu (Management).
- rt-portal connects to afw-hub (Private network: peering).
- pe-sql-portal connects to sqldb-portal (Private network: TDS).
- pe-kv-portal signs in with kv-portal-prd (secrets).
- appi-portal connects to log-platform-weu (Management: workspace).
- sqldb-portal connects to log-platform-weu (Management).
- kv-portal-prd connects to log-platform-weu (Management).
- func-portal-jobs connects to pe-sql-portal (Private network) and connects to log-platform-weu (Management).

Walkthrough
1. One subscription for the customer portal, with a resource group per concern.
2. Out through the hub: The app's outbound traffic leaves through its integration subnet, whose route table sends it to the hub firewall.
3. Data stays private: SQL and Key Vault have public access off. The app reaches them through private endpoints, with its managed identity.
4. Monitored centrally: Every resource sends its diagnostics to the platform workspace (the badge on each card); App Insights stores its data there too.

Key
