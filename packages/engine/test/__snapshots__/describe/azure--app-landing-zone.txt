Application landing zone

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind customer portal · one subscription, a resource group per concern, plugged into the platform. 10 elements in 10 groups, with 14 connections.

Groups and elements
- sub-portal-prod (Landing zone · Corp)
  - rg-portal-app (Resource group)
    - app-portal-prod (Managed identity)
    - func-portal-prod (Jobs · managed identity)
  - rg-portal-network (Resource group)
    - vnet-portal-prod (10.1.12.0/22)
      - snet-app (10.1.12.0/24 · VNet integration)
        - rt-portal-prod (0.0.0.0/0 → 10.0.0.68)
      - snet-pe (10.1.13.0/24)
        - pep-sql-portal-prod (10.1.13.4)
        - pep-kv-portal-prod (10.1.13.5)
  - rg-portal-monitor (Resource group)
    - appi-portal-prod (Application Insights)
  - rg-portal-data (Resource group)
    - sql-portal-prod (Database sqldb-portal-prod)
    - kv-portal-prod (Key Vault)
- sub-connectivity (Platform)
  - afw-hub-weu (Azure Firewall Premium · 10.0.0.68)
- sub-management (Platform)
  - log-platform-weu (Central workspace)

Connections
- app-portal-prod connects to rt-portal-prod, connects to afw-hub-weu (Private network: outbound), connects to pep-sql-portal-prod (Private network), connects to pep-kv-portal-prod (Private network), sends data to appi-portal-prod and connects to log-platform-weu (Management).
- rt-portal-prod connects to afw-hub-weu (next hop).
- pep-sql-portal-prod connects to sql-portal-prod (Private network: TDS).
- pep-kv-portal-prod connects to kv-portal-prod (Private network: HTTPS).
- appi-portal-prod sends data to log-platform-weu (workspace).
- sql-portal-prod connects to log-platform-weu (Management).
- kv-portal-prod connects to log-platform-weu (Management).
- func-portal-prod connects to pep-sql-portal-prod (Private network) and connects to log-platform-weu (Management).

Walkthrough
1. One subscription: The customer portal in a subscription of its own, with a resource group per concern.
2. Out through the hub: Outbound traffic leaves through the integration subnet; its route table sends it to the hub firewall.
3. Data stays private: SQL and Key Vault have no public access; the app uses private endpoints and its managed identity.
4. Monitored centrally: Every resource, App Insights too, sends its data to the platform workspace (the badge on each card).

Key
- Dotted line: Route table (configuration, not traffic)
- Dashed teal line: Private network
- Solid violet line with ticks: Data flow
- Badge: Management badge
