Sequence, dead-lettered and resubmitted: Dead-lettered and resubmitted. 4 participants and 4 messages.

Participants
- Service Bus sb-northwind-prod
  - order-events (topic)
  - Dead-letter queue (per subscription)
- Azure Monitor (alert rule)
- Operations

Message flow
In order, top to bottom.
- 9. Azure Monitor sends to Operations: Alert: dead-lettered > 0.
- 10. Operations calls Dead-letter queue: Peek the dead-lettered message.
- 11. Dead-letter queue replies to Operations: OrderPlaced, reason: MaxDeliveryCountExceeded.
- 12. Operations sends to order-events: Fix the cause, resubmit.

Key
- Arrow with a filled head: Call
- Dashed arrow: Reply
- Arrow with an open head: Async message
