Events through Service Bus

Sequence, dead-lettered and resubmitted: Dead-lettered and resubmitted. 6 participants and 8 messages.

Participants
- Service Bus sb-northwind-prod
  - order-events (topic)
  - Dead-letter queue (per subscription)
- Invoicing (Azure Function)
- Warehouse (Container App)
- Azure Monitor (alert rule)
- Operations

Message flow
In order, top to bottom.
- 10. Azure Monitor sends to Operations: Alert: dead-lettered > 0.
- 11. Operations calls Dead-letter queue: Peek the dead-lettered message.
- 12. Dead-letter queue replies to Operations: OrderPlaced, reason: MaxDeliveryCountExceeded.
- 13. Operations sends to order-events: Fix the cause, resubmit with a new MessageId.
- 14. order-events sends to Invoicing: OrderPlaced again.
- 15. Invoicing replies to order-events: Complete, invoiced already.
- 16. order-events sends to Warehouse: OrderPlaced again.
- 17. Warehouse replies to order-events: Complete, stock reserved.

Key
- Arrow with a filled head: Call
- Dashed arrow: Reply
- Arrow with an open head: Async message
