Unity Catalog access

Roles, groups and access packages diagram: Northwind lakehouse · account groups from Entra ID, grants per domain and environment. 17 elements in 2 groups, with 19 connections.

Groups and elements
- platform-team (Metastore admins)
- uc-weu (Metastore)
- orders-team (Account group)
- engineers (Every domain team)
- orders_dev (Bound to dev)
- orders_test (Bound to test)
- orders_prod (Bound to prod)
- customers-team (Account group)
- customers_prod (Bound to prod)
  - customers_prod.gold (pii tagged)
- analysts (Power BI and Genie)
- sales_prod (Bound to prod)
  - sales_prod.gold (sell_through)
- sales-team (Account group)
- contoso_sell_through (Share)
- sp-lakehouse-dev (Runs the jobs in dev)
- sp-lakehouse-test (Runs the jobs in test)
- sp-lakehouse-prod (Runs the jobs in prod)
- contoso (OpenSharing recipient)

Connections
- platform-team is Metastore admin on uc-weu.
- orders-team is a member of engineers, is Owner on orders_dev, is Read on orders_test and is MANAGE on orders_prod.
- engineers is BROWSE on orders_prod.
- customers-team is a member of engineers and is MANAGE on customers_prod.
- analysts is Read · pii masked on customers_prod.gold and is Read on sales_prod.gold.
- sales-team is a member of engineers, is MANAGE on sales_prod and is Owner on contoso_sell_through.
- sp-lakehouse-dev is Write on orders_dev.
- sp-lakehouse-test is Write on orders_test.
- sp-lakehouse-prod is Write on orders_prod, is Write on customers_prod and is Write on sales_prod.
- contoso is SELECT on share on contoso_sell_through.

Walkthrough
1. Groups come from Entra ID: Automatic identity management syncs Entra groups, nested ones too, to the account.
2. The platform team administers: platform-team is metastore admin (and account admin); it holds no grants on domain data.
3. One domain, three environments: orders-team owns dev, reads test, and in prod reads and MANAGEs, so it decides who reads.
4. Only service principals write: Jobs run as their environment's service principal; catalogs are bound to one workspace.
5. Personal data masked: An ABAC policy masks columns tagged pii; analysts see the rows, not names and emails.
6. Shared with Contoso: sales-team owns the share; Contoso reads sell_through in its own Databricks account.
