Connections
- CA001 Admins applies to sg-entra-tier0-admins, applies to sg-entra-exo-admins, applies to sg-entra-spo-admins, excludes Break-glass accounts, requires PAW and requires Phishing-resistant MFA.
- sg-entra-tier0-admins can activate Global Administrator on Northwind for at most 2 h, with approval by sg-entra-tier0-admins; can activate Privileged Role Administrator on Northwind for at most 2 h, with approval by sg-entra-tier0-admins.

- sg-entra-spo-admins can activate SharePoint Administrator on Northwind for at most 8 h.

- sg-helpdesk-nl is Helpdesk Administrator on AU Netherlands and can activate User Administrator on AU Netherlands.

Walkthrough
1. Three tiers: A role-assignable group per tier. Tier 0 controls the tenant, Tier 1 manages services such as mail and sites, Tier 2 supports users.
2. Break-glass: Two cloud-only accounts hold Global Administrator permanently, for when sign-in or PIM fails. Left out of Conditional Access, FIDO2 keys in a safe.
  - Break-glass accounts: Alert on every sign-in
3. Admins sign in safely: One Conditional Access policy covers every admin group. It requires a privileged access workstation and a phishing-resistant method, and leaves out the break-glass accounts.
4. No standing Tier 0: Tier 0 admins use a separate admin account. Global Administrator is eligible only, for two hours at most, and a second Tier 0 admin approves each activation.
5. Tier 1, just in time: Exchange and SharePoint admins activate their own role, with MFA and a reason, for at most 8 hours. Security and Intune admins count as Tier 0.
6. Tier 2, scoped: The Dutch service desk supports only the users in its unit. Admins are in role-assignable groups, so it cannot reset them.
