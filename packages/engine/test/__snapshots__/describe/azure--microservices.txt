Order platform

Azure infrastructure diagram: Event-driven microservices on Container Apps. 11 elements in 2 groups, with 14 connections.

Groups and elements
- Production (Subscription)
  - rg-orders (Resource group)
    - Public API (API Management)
    - Orders (Container App)
    - Inventory (Container App)
    - order-events (Service Bus)
    - Orders store (Cosmos DB)
    - Secrets (Key Vault)
    - Telemetry (Application Insights)
    - Inventory DB (SQL Database)
    - Payments (Container App)
    - Notifications (Function App)
- Payment provider (External system)

Connections
- Public API connects to Orders (Private network) and connects to Inventory (Private network).
- Orders sends data to order-events (OrderPlaced), connects to Orders store (Private network), signs in with Secrets and connects to Telemetry (Management).
- Inventory connects to Telemetry (Management) and connects to Inventory DB (Private network).
- order-events sends data to Inventory, sends data to Payments and sends data to Notifications.
- Payments signs in with Secrets, connects to Telemetry (Management) and sends traffic to Payment provider (REST).

Key
- Solid sky line: Public traffic
- Dashed teal line: Private network
- Solid violet line with ticks: Data flow
- Amber badge: Reached with its managed identity
- Badge: Management badge
