Finance domain

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind lakehouse · invoices from the ERP into finance_prod, reported in Power BI. 9 elements in 2 groups, with 8 connections.

Groups and elements
- ERP (SQL Server · on-premises): Orders and invoices and stock
- erp-ingest (SQL Server connector · ingestion gateway)
- finance_prod (Catalog)
  - bronze (Schema)
  - silver (Schema)
  - gold (Schema)
    - revenue (Table)
- bi-warehouse (Serverless SQL warehouse)
- Power BI (Fabric capacity F64): Reports on gold through the SQL warehouse
- Business analysts (Power BI · Genie): Read gold data in Power BI and Genie
- finance-pipeline (Lakeflow pipeline)

Connections
- ERP sends data to erp-ingest (CDC [SQL Server]).
- erp-ingest sends data to bronze.
- bronze sends data to silver (clean).
- silver sends data to gold (aggregate).
- gold sends data to bi-warehouse.
- bi-warehouse sends data to Power BI (DirectQuery).
- Power BI sends data to Business analysts (Reports).
- finance-pipeline connects to finance_prod (Management: refreshes).

Key
- Solid violet line with ticks: Data flow
- Dotted line with ticks: Management
