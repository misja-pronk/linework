Kestrel Online — containers

C4 model: What runs, and how the parts talk. 7 elements in 1 group, with 7 connections.

Groups and elements
- Personal customer (Person): Holds accounts at Kestrel Bank
- Kestrel Online (Software System): Lets customers check balances, move money and manage their cards
  - Web banking (Container: Next.js): Online banking in the browser
  - Mobile app (Container: Kotlin and Swift): Balances, transfers and cards
  - Banking API (Container: Spring Boot): Sign-in, accounts and payments as JSON
  - Customer store (Container: PostgreSQL 16): Logins, devices and sessions
  - Account events (Container: Kafka): Payments and sign-ins as they happen
- Core ledger (Software System): Holds every account and booking

Connections
- Personal customer connects to Web banking (Banks online [HTTPS]) and connects to Mobile app (Banks on the phone).
- Web banking connects to Banking API (Calls [JSON/HTTPS]).
- Mobile app connects to Banking API (Calls [JSON/HTTPS]).
- Banking API connects to Customer store (Reads logins [JDBC]), connects to Core ledger (Reads and books [ISO 20022 over MQ]) and sends messages to Account events (Publishes [Kafka]).
