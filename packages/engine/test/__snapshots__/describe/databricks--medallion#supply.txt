Supply chain domain

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind lakehouse · stock from the ERP and the suppliers' files over SFTP into supply_prod. 7 elements in 3 groups, with 6 connections.

Groups and elements
- ERP (SQL Server · on-premises): Orders and invoices and stock
- erp-ingest (SQL Server connector · ingestion gateway)
- supply_prod (Catalog)
  - bronze (Schema)
    - supplier_files (Table)
  - silver (Schema)
  - gold (Schema)
    - stock_levels (Table)
- Supplier SFTP (SFTP server · CSV files): Stock and lead times that suppliers upload
- supply-pipeline (SFTP connector · hourly)

Connections
- ERP sends data to erp-ingest (CDC [SQL Server]).
- erp-ingest sends data to bronze.
- bronze sends data to silver (clean).
- silver sends data to gold (model).
- Supplier SFTP sends data to bronze over SFTP connector · CSV.
- supply-pipeline connects to supply_prod (Management: refreshes).

Key
- Solid violet line with ticks: Data flow
- Dotted line with ticks: Management
