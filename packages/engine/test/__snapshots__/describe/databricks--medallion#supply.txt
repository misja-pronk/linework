Supply chain domain

Azure infrastructure diagram: Northwind lakehouse · stock and supplier files into supply_prod, a demand forecast served to replenishment. 11 elements in 3 groups, with 10 connections.

Groups and elements
- ERP (SQL Server · on-premises): Orders and invoices and stock
- erp-ingest (SQL Server connector · ingestion gateway)
- supply_prod (Catalog)
  - bronze (Schema)
    - supplier_files (Volume)
  - silver (Schema)
  - gold (Schema)
    - demand_forecast (Model)
    - stock_levels (Table)
- forecast-training (Weekly · MLflow)
- demand-forecast (Serving endpoint)
- Replenishment (Supply planning app): Orders stock from suppliers on the demand forecast
- Supplier SFTP (SFTP server · CSV files): Stock and lead times that suppliers upload
- supply-pipeline (Auto Loader · hourly)

Connections
- ERP sends data to erp-ingest (CDC [SQL Server]).
- erp-ingest sends data to bronze.
- bronze sends data to silver (clean).
- silver sends data to gold (model).
- gold sends data to forecast-training.
- forecast-training sends data to demand_forecast (Registers).
- demand_forecast sends data to demand-forecast (Served by).
- demand-forecast sends data to Replenishment (Forecast [REST]).
- Supplier SFTP sends data to bronze over SFTP · Auto Loader.
- supply-pipeline connects to supply_prod (Management: refreshes).

Key
- Solid violet line with ticks: Data flow
- Dotted line with ticks: Management
